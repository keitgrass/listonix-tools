Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

FixMyASIN is a trading name of Foodel OÜ, a company registered in Estonia (Kosemetsa 13-3, 12012 Tallinn, Estonia). Contact: hello@fixmyasin.com. Foodel OÜ is the data controller for personal data collected on fixmyasin.com.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name and email when you use a form, order or email us.
  • Listing and ad data: the ASIN, listing text, product facts and PPC reports you send us.
  • Payment details: processed by Stripe. We see your name, email and payment status, never your full card number.

Why we use it

  • To deliver the service you ordered and to reply to you (contract).
  • To send you a free listing score result and an occasional follow-up about your listing (legitimate interest). You can opt out at any time by replying "unsubscribe".
  • To keep accounting records (legal obligation).

Who processes it for us

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Netlify (website hosting)
  • Formspree (form submissions)
  • Anthropic (AI analysis of the listing text you submit; not used to train models)
  • Google (email)
  • Microsoft Clarity (site analytics, only if you accept cookies)

Some of these providers are in the United States and use standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. We don't sell your data.

How long we keep it

Order and listing data: up to 3 years after your last order, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Accounting records: as long as Estonian law requires (currently 7 years).

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, deletion, or to object to processing by emailing hello@fixmyasin.com. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee).

Cookies and analytics

fixmyasin.com does not use advertising cookies. If you click Accept on our cookie banner, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site (clicks, scrolling and anonymized session recordings). Text you type into forms is masked and is not recorded. Clarity sets cookies and processes this data on our behalf; see Microsoft's privacy statement. If you decline, Clarity is not loaded. You can change your choice any time: cookie settings.

Your browser also stores your cookie choice and, if you buy a report, your last report locally on your own device so you can reopen it.
