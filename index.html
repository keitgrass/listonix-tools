What we need from you

We keep it simple. Here's everything, so there's no back-and-forth.

For the PPC audit

  1. Search term report, last 60 daysAmazon Ads → Reports → Create report → Sponsored Products → Search term → Download
  2. Your bulk file (optional, makes the audit sharper)Campaign Manager → Bulk operations → Create spreadsheet → Download
  3. Price and profit margin of the products, so we know your break-even ACoS

For managed PPC

  1. User access to Amazon AdsSettings → Manage access → Invite user. We'll send you the email to invite. You can remove access anytime.
  2. Products to advertise (ASINs) and their price and margin
  3. Monthly ad budget you're comfortable with
  4. Your main goal: launch and rank, grow profit, or clear inventory

Ad spend is paid by you directly to Amazon; we never handle your payment details. Results depend on your product, price and competition, so we don't guarantee specific ACoS or sales figures.
